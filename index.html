Project Overview

For this project, my task was to take a word from a foreign language that doesn't have a direct translation in English and design a booklet for it. Out of the words I could choose, I decided to use the Japanese word "Komorebi". This word roughly translates to "sunlight leaking through trees", and is used to describe the beauty of light rays bleeding and seeping between the leaves of the canopy of a tree/forest. I had a lot of enjoyment studying the word and designing my spreads. I felt like having access to another language allowed for more creativity within the design. Since I'm also learning Japanese, I was able to apply some of the bits of knowledge I have about the language to this project. I tried to make a Japanese inspired booklet that uses Japanese bookbinding and watercolor paper as the cover pages. I incorporated yellow into the book's visuals to mimic the idea of sunlight.
